# ADR-0006.5 — Tiered frontend gateway: generated types without flattened streams

**Status:** proposed
**Date:** 2026-08-20
**Supersedes:** ADR-0006

## Context

ADR-0006 correctly rejects hand-written copies of Flutter-Rust bridge DTOs and
redundant feature data layers. Its collection migration, however, cannot mean
that Flutter receives one ever-growing `AppSnapshot`: the generated contract
has three deliberately different costs.

- `AppSnapshot` is the inexpensive application-wide summary.
- `AppDetail` is selected-collection data: entries, piece map, and live peers.
- The history stream is append-only transfer readings, because re-sending a
  complete history ring to append one reading is wasted bridge work.

A literal single snapshot stream would reintroduce the cost the Rust API was
designed to avoid. Keeping separate repositories for those streams would make
the opposite mistake: more test seams and more paths to trace.

## Decision

Flutter has one fakeable **`NexusGateway`** facade. It owns the generated
snapshot, detail, and history streams plus generated `AppCommand` dispatch.
Generated FRB types are the frontend domain types; no collection, media,
history, peer, or command DTO mirrors are introduced.

Collection presentation consumes `AppCollection`, `AppDetail`, and `AppEntry`
directly. Display-only meanings—status labels, byte formatting, image
eligibility, and decoded history readings—live in extensions or pure
presentation helpers. They may derive values but must not retain a second copy
of backend state.

## Why (pattern)

**Facade with tiered read models.** The gateway is the one substitutable
boundary for tests; the read models stay tiered because their data has
different fan-out, size, and cadence. This preserves ADR-0001's single
`AppSnapshot` + command boundary without turning a selected detail or history
stream into global state.

## Consequences

- `NexusGateway` replaces pass-through repository naming and is the only FFI
  fake in Flutter tests.
- `AppCommand` replaces any hand-written command envelope; a small factory may
  fill mandatory empty generated fields without creating another type.
- `Collection`, `collectionView`, `CollectionSource`, `EngineCollectionSource`,
  `MediaItem`, `TransferHistory`, and `PeerObservation` are retired as direct
  generated values reach their presentation consumers.
- Widgets subscribe to selected detail/history through the gateway or their
  controller-owned state, never by caching a duplicate application model.
- The frontend remains lean while preserving the Rust API's summary/detail/
  append-only-history performance boundaries.
